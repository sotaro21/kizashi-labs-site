AI agents that read the market.

Yomi (読み) means "reading ahead," as a shogi player reads the moves to come. We build an autonomous research pipeline that turns market data into forecasts, checks every forecast against reality, and keeps a human in charge of every trade.

What we're building

Research

Claude-powered agents collect and summarize market data, filings and news every trading day.

Forecast

Daily price forecasts for Japanese and US equities, generated before the market opens.

Verify

Every forecast is scored against the actual close. Accuracy is tracked over time, not assumed.

Strategy

Backtested strategies built from verified signals, with broker API integration next.

How we work

Automation does the research. People make the decisions. No order is ever placed without explicit human approval.

Our stack is built on the Claude API and Claude Code: multi-agent workflows, scheduled cloud routines, and a long-term knowledge base that the agents maintain themselves.
